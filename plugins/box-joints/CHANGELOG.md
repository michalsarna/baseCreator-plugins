# Changelog
## 1.0.0
* First version, for LightCreator: a box to cut out of plywood, acrylic or card and put together without glue. Every face is a separate piece and the edges have fingers and notches (a box joint) that fit into each other.
* The same shapes as in `box-nets`: a prism or a truncated prism (slanted top) on a triangle, rectangle, trapezoid or regular polygon, a pyramid on a triangle or rectangle, and on a circle or an ellipse a cylinder (straight or slanted), a cone and a truncated cone. The same pictures show which length is which.
* The sizes are the outer sizes of the box. The fingers are as deep as the material is thick (on a slanted joint as deep as the other piece reaches: the angle between the faces is worked out from the corners of the solid); the finger width, the kerf of the laser and the layer are set in the form; the lid can be left out.
* A round wall is a strip (a fan for a cone) closed by a comb along its ends, the discs have fingers along the rim.
* The pieces are packed in rows in the middle of the work area and inserted as one group.
