# Changelog
## 2.1.0
* A cylinder: a prism on a circle or an ellipse (the new bases Circle and Ellipse), straight or cut slantways (Truncated prism, the top rises towards the back along the semi-axis b). The wall is a strip as long as the rim; the bottom and the top are joined to it in the middle by a short chord (one piece), each with a ring of glue tabs. Pictures with r, or a and b.

## 2.0.0
* The net is one piece where the shape allows it: the bottom and the top are folded on to the strip of the sides (the fan of a pyramid) along an edge that gives an outline without overlaps, so there is one outline to cut and the rest are dashed folds. The glue tabs sit on the other edges of the bottom and the top, one closes the sides. When no edge works, that polygon is a separate piece (the plugin says so).
* The result is inserted as one group.
* Pictures beside the form show the chosen shape with the lengths marked; the labels use the same letters. (Needs baseCreator 1.11.0: the generator window now also has a preview and closes after Create.)
* The longest side of a triangle and the longer base of a trapezoid lie at the bottom.

## 1.0.0
* First version: the net of a prism, a truncated prism or a pyramid, with the outline continuous, the folds dashed and glue tabs. For PenCreator.
